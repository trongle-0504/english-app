

## Các dạng câu hỏi

### Yes / No questions

<table class="w-full border-collapse text-base">
	<tbody>
		<tr>
			<td class="border border-black bg-emerald-50/70 p-2 font-semibold" rowspan="2">        Yes     </td>
			<td class="border border-black p-2"><strong>100%</strong></td>
			<td class="border border-black p-2">For sure / Definitely / Totally / Certainly / Without a doubt</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td class="border border-black p-2"><strong>60-70</strong></td>
			<td class="border border-black p-2">Kind of / Probably / A little bit / I guess so / I would say so</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td class="border border-black bg-rose-50/70 p-2 font-semibold" rowspan="3">No</td>
			<td class="border border-black p-2"><strong>50/50</strong></td>
			<td class="border border-black p-2">I'm not sure / It's really hard to say / Maybe / I'm on the fence about this</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td class="border border-black p-2"><strong>- 60-70</strong></td>
			<td class="border border-black p-2">Not really / I wouldn't say so / I guess not / Probably not / I wouldn't go out of my way to say yes</td>
		</tr>
		<tr>
			<td class="border border-black p-2"><strong>- 100</strong></td>
			<td class="border border-black p-2">Not at all / Definitely not / Certainly not / I don't think so / The answer is a definite no</td>
		</tr>
	</tbody>
</table>


**Mức độ 100%**

* Definitely / certainly $\leftrightarrow$ Definitely not/ certainly not / the answer is a definite no
* totally $\leftrightarrow$ not at all

**Mức độ 60 - 70%**

* I guess so $\leftrightarrow$ I guess not
* Probably $\leftrightarrow$ probably not
* I would say so $\leftrightarrow$ I wouldn't say so

<details class="group my-3 rounded-xl border border-slate-200 bg-slate-50/80 p-3 shadow-sm dark:border-slate-700 dark:bg-slate-900/40">
<summary class="cursor-pointer font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-100">Example</summary>
<div class="mt-2 space-y-1">

1. Do you think smartphones are important in daily life?  
**Definitely.** I use my phone every day for work, maps, and chatting with friends.

2. Do you think you can live without the internet nowadays?  
**Definitely not.** Almost everything now depends on the internet.

3. Will it rain this afternoon?  
**I guess so.** The sky looks really dark right now.

4. Do you think Tom will join the football match tomorrow?  
**I guess not.** He said he was feeling sick today.

5. Will your parents enjoy this restaurant?  
**Probably.** They usually like Vietnamese food.

6. Will the shop still be open at 11 p.m.?  
**Probably not.** Most shops here close quite early.

7. Is learning English useful for students?  
**I would say so.** It helps students study and work internationally.

8. Is coffee good for children?  
**I wouldn't say so.** Too much caffeine isn't healthy for them.

</div>
</details>

<div class="mt-2 space-y-1">

**1. It would be nothing / no one else if not other than + N**

1. Which field are you the most passionate about?  
It would be **nothing else if not other than** artificial intelligence.

2. Who inspires you the most in your academic journey?  
It would be **no one else other than** my research advisor.

3. If you could study abroad in one country, which one would you choose?  
It would be **none other than** Poland.


**2. It would be + to V**

4. What would be the best way to improve English speaking skills?  
**It would be to practice** consistently on a daily basis.

5. What is your biggest goal right now?  
**It would be to secure** a PhD scholarship abroad.

6. What would be your dream career?  
**It would be to become** an AI researcher.


**3. It would be a couple of things / people, one of which is + N / to V**

7. What helps students succeed academically?  
It would be **a couple of things, one of which is** maintaining self-discipline.

8. Who has had the greatest influence on your life?  
It would be **a couple of people, one of whom is** my university professor.

9. What helps you stay productive throughout the day?  
It would be **a couple of habits, one of which is** planning my tasks in advance.

</div>
</details>

### Where / when

<div class="rounded-2xl border border-amber-100 bg-amber-50 p-4 shadow-sm dark:border-amber-800/50 dark:bg-amber-900/20">


**--Present--**

I (often) V / do it + **[place] / [time]** + when **SV** / where **SV**

**[place] / [time]** + when **SV** / where **SV** (MĐQH) + is (usually) a good place / time for me to V / do this

**--Past--**

If i remember correctly, it was **[place] / [time]**

</div>

<div class="mt-2 space-y-1">

**Mẫu 1: I (often) V / do it + [place] / [time] + when SV / where SV**

1. When do you usually work on your AI projects?  
I often work on my AI projects **last thing before bed** **when there are fewer distractions around me**.

2. Where do you usually study for IELTS?  
I usually study for IELTS **in the comfort of my room** **where it is just me and myself**.

3. When do you normally read research papers?  
I normally read research papers **at the crack of dawn** **when I am the first one up**.

4. Where do you usually think about your future plans?  
I usually think about my future plans **outside of the house** **where it is tranquil and calm**.

5. When do you usually fix bugs in your code?  
I often fix bugs in my code **when I have some time to spare** after finishing my main tasks.

**Mẫu 2: [place] / [time] + when SV / where SV + is (usually) a good place / time for me to V / do this**

6. Where is a good place for you to focus on coding?  
**A place where it is quiet and where there are not too many people in the background** is usually a good place for me to focus on coding.

7. When is a good time for you to practice English speaking?  
**First thing in the morning when my mind is still fresh** is usually a good time for me to practice English speaking.

8. Where is a good place for you to come up with research ideas?  
**A café out and about where it is lively and full of energy** is usually a good place for me to come up with research ideas.

9. When is a good time for you to plan your day?  
**Last thing before bed when I am the last one to turn in** is usually a good time for me to plan my day.

10. Where is a good place for you to think deeply about life?  
**A quiet park where it is just me and myself** is usually a good place for me to think deeply about life.

<details class="group my-3 rounded-xl border border-slate-200 bg-slate-50/80 p-3 shadow-sm dark:border-slate-700 dark:bg-slate-900/40">
<summary class="cursor-pointer font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-100">Example</summary>
<div class="mt-2 space-y-1">

1. Why do many people hate traveling in groups?  
→ Travelling in groups can **prevent people from having** freedom.

2. Why do many people hate living in the city?  
→ Living in big cities can **make people suffer** from noise and pollution.

3. Why do some people dislike traveling abroad?  
→ Travelling abroad can **cost a lot of money to travel** and explore different places.

4. Do you like going to the gym? Why not?  
→ No, because working out at the gym can **make people feel** exhausted and **take a lot of time to maintain** regularly.

5. Do you like eating fast food? Why not?  
→ No, because eating too much fast food can **increase the risk of obesity** and other health issues.

6. Do you think it is necessary for young kids to learn a second language? Why not?  
→ Learning a second language at an early age can **take a lot of time and effort to master** and may **distract children from focusing** on other important subjects.

7. Do you think children should be allowed to use computers freely? Why not?  
→ Using computers freely can **discourage children from studying** and **increase the risk of becoming** addicted to online games.

8. Why is it important to protect the environment?  
→ Protecting the environment can **reduce the risk of natural disasters**.

9. Why do many students dislike doing group projects?  
→ Group projects can **give students no choice but to work** with irresponsible teammates.

10. Why do some people avoid driving in big cities?  
→ Heavy traffic can **stop people from getting** to work on time.

11. Why do many people dislike office jobs?  
→ Office jobs often **require people to sit** for long hours every day.

12. Why do some parents limit their children’s screen time?
