#### Sample Answer

It is these humorous and relatable visuals that prompt them to copy, thereby helping them absorb knowledge and boost their intelligence more effectively.

Now, when it comes to grown-ups, you've got to figure out where they like to hang out. Are they always on Facebook, watching TV, or maybe they're into those online chat places? It's like picking the perfect spot for a good talk. And for adults, they're all about getting something useful. Show them how your thing can fix a problem or make their life easier, and you'll have their attention in no time. It's all about finding what clicks with them.

*   **fix a problem**: giải quyết vấn đề
*   **have their attention in no time**: thu hút sự chú ý của họ ngay lập tức
