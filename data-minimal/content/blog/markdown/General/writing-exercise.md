
## Attend University

**These days, more and more people are choosing to enter higher education.**
**Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?**

| 🏫 Advantages | 💰 Disadvantages |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Career advancement**: Enter higher education → gain specialized knowledge → perform better at work + get promoted → further advance their careers.<br/><br/>*Vào học cao hơn → tích lũy kiến thức chuyên ngành → làm việc tốt hơn + được thăng tiến → thăng tiến hơn nữa trong sự nghiệp.* | **Cost**: Obtain a higher education → bear the very expensive cost of college → have to use government/federal loans or private loans in order to pay for their education → end up in debt for the rest of their life.<br/><br/>*Học cao hơn → phải chi trả chi phí học đại học rất đắt đỏ → phải sử dụng các khoản vay của chính phủ/liên bang hoặc các khoản vay tư nhân để chi trả cho việc học của mình → mắc nợ đến hết đời.* |
| **Decision making**: Be better educated → increase people’s knowledge and cognitive skills → make improved decisions in regards to their health, healthcare and managing their medical care.<br/><br/>*Được giáo dục tốt hơn → nâng cao kiến thức và kỹ năng nhận thức của mọi người → đưa ra quyết định cải thiện liên quan đến sức khỏe, chăm sóc sức khỏe và quản lý chăm sóc y tế của họ.* | **Skill gap**: Attend a four year institution of higher education → not prepare students with hands-on skills that are beneficial for their career → put them at a disadvantage on the job market.<br/><br/>*Theo học tại một cơ sở giáo dục đại học bốn năm → không chuẩn bị cho sinh viên các kỹ năng thực hành có lợi cho sự nghiệp của họ → đặt họ vào thế bất lợi trên thị trường việc làm.* |

### Sample Essay

Choosing to enter higher education is becoming more popular.

**While this can offer various advantages, it can also bring about some detrimental effects.**

**On the one hand, there are several benefits** of choosing to enter higher education.

**The most obvious advantage is that** pursuing a university degree enables students to acquire specialized knowledge. This can help students perform better at work, which increases their chances of getting promoted. As a result, pursuing higher education can further advance their careers.

**Another merit worth mentioning is that** entering higher education allows individuals to become better educated. This helps to increase people’s knowledge and cognitive skills. Consequently, students can make more informed decisions in regards to their health, healthcare and managing their medical care.

**On the other hand, the drawbacks of** choosing to enter higher education **are undeniable.**

**The first disadvantage is that** obtaining a degree requires students to bear a very expensive cost of college. This often leads them to use government or federal loans or private loans in order to pay for their education, which can result in ending up in debt for the rest of their life.

**The second downside is that** attending a four-year institution of higher education may not adequately prepare students with hands-on skills that are beneficial for their careers. This can put them at a disadvantage in the job market.

**In conclusion, although** choosing to enter higher education **can be beneficial in some situations, this development can also be disadvantageous to a certain extent.**

## Increasing life expectancy

In the developed world, average life expectancy is increasing. 

What problems will this cause for individuals and society? Suggest some measures that could be taken to reduce the impact of aging populations.

### Sample Outline

**1st Problem:** an increase in the number of elderly individuals in a family → other family members shoulder responsibility for caring for the elderly → not have enough time for work and personal activities → suffer from stress and burnout

**2nd Problem:** an increase in the elderly population → place greater burden on social security and pension systems → a decrease in funds for other government programs/sectors in the society → make economic growth stagnate

**1st solution:** build more facilities for the elderly such as nursing homes → take better care of the elderly → alleviate the burden for their family members → family members focus on work or other personal activities


**2nd solution:** increase retirement age → elderly employees remain in the workplace longer → reduce financial burden on social security and pension programs.

### Sample Essay

**It is true that** there is an increase in the average life expectancy in the developed world. **This can produce some detrimental effects, which require possible solutions to be adopted.**

**There are many issues stemming from the increase in the number of the elderly individuals in society.** **One obvious problem is that** other family members have to shoulder responsibility for caring for the elderly, **and therefore** they do not have enough time for work and personal activities. **As a result**, they are more likely to lead a less comfortable life, and eventually suffer from stress and burnout. **Another negative effect is that** an increase in the elderly population will place a greater burden on the social security and pension system. **Consequently**, this leads to the decreased availability of funds for other government programs, which makes economic growth stagnate.

**Despite these problems, various actions can be taken to mitigate the situation.** 
**The first possible measure is that** the government should have plans to build more facilities for the elderly, such as nursing homes, so these facilities can take better care of the elderly. **Therefore**, this can alleviate the burden for other family members, which allows them to focus on their work or personal activities. **The second approach is that** the government should also increase the retirement age. **This results in** the fact that elderly employees remain in the workplace longer, **and thus** helps reduce burden on social security and pension programs.

**In conclusion, although the increase in average life expectancy can give rise to many consequences, several steps can be taken to combat the situation.**

## Difficulties in Higher Education


### Sample Outline

#### Causes

**1. Higher education**
 involves a higher level of complexity and depth compared to school curricula
→ requires students to do extensive reading and research after class
→ helps students gain a deeper understanding of complex concepts
→ makes it more demanding for students to thrive in university

**2. University students**
 do not have a structured schedule or close supervision from teachers
→ need to learn how to manage their own time and seek help when necessary
→ often struggle to balance academic responsibilities and social activities
→ may experience hindered study progress as a result

#### Solutions

**1. Establish academic advising services**
 provide personalized guidance for students
→ address individual learning needs
→ enhance student support
**Example:**
 Some Vietnamese students consult their tutors about reliable sources of information
→ they can find relevant articles and books for self-study
→ this facilitates their learning process

**2. Universities and colleges should implement study skills workshops**
 provide strategies for effective learning and time management
→ enhance student preparedness
→ improve academic performance

### Sample Essay

**It is true that** many students encounter greater difficulty in studying during their university or college years compared to their time in school. **This can stem from many causes, which requires possible solutions to be adopted.**

**There are some reasons for** the challenges students may face when entering tertiary education. **Perhaps one of these is that** higher education involves a higher level of complexity and depth in subjects compared to school curricula. This requires students to do extensive reading and research after class to have a deep understanding of complex concepts, **which is why** they may find it more demanding to thrive in university. **Another possible explanation is that** university students do not have a structured schedule and close supervision from teachers. **Therefore**, they have to learn to manage their own time, and seek help when needed. **This may make** them struggle to strike a balance between academic responsibilities and social activities, which can hinder their study progress.

**However, various actions can be taken to mitigate the situation.** **The first possible measure is that** schools can establish academic advising services which can provide students with personalized guidance. **This can help** address their individual learning needs and thus enhance student support. Some Vietnamese students, **for example**, can consult their tutors about the sources of information where they can find relevant articles and books for self-study, which helps facilitate their learning. **The second approach is that** universities and colleges can also implement study skills workshops. **As a result**, students can be provided with strategies for effective learning and time management. This helps enhance student preparedness, **resulting in** improved academic performance.

**In conclusion, although** the difficulty of transitioning from school to university or college can result from many factors, **several steps can be taken to combat the situation.**

## Too much time on computers

**Many children spend too much time on computers.**
**What problems can this cause? What can be the solutions?**

### Sample Outline

**1st Problem:**
- an increase in the number of elderly individuals in a family  
→ other family members shoulder the responsibility for caring for the elderly  
→ not have enough time for work and personal activities  
→ suffer from stress and burnout  

**2nd Problem:**
- children spend too much time playing video games  
→ have less time for studying  
→ fail to meet deadlines or finish homework on time  
→ get lower scores at school  

**1st Solution:**
- schools organize educational programs about responsible computer use  
→ children raise awareness of the harmful effects of excessive computer use  
→ manage their schedules more effectively  
→ reduce the amount of time spent on computers  

**2nd Solution:**
- parents monitor screen time and applications their children use  
→ install parental control software  
→ restrict access to entertainment applications  
→ children spend more time on academic and outdoor activities  

### Sample Essay

**It is true that** it is common for many children to spend too much time on computers.  
**This can produce some detrimental effects**, which requires possible solutions to be adopted.  

**There are many issues stemming from** spending too much time on computers.  
**One obvious problem is that** children may spend too much time playing video games on computers, which have less time for studying. **Consequently**, they fail to meet deadlines or finish their homework on time. **As a result**, they are likely to get lower scores at school.  

**Another negative effect is that** children can access a wide variety of entertainment apps such as games, music streaming apps and online dating platforms. This may lead to them spending less time interacting with one other. **As a result**, this can drive a wedge between parents and children.  

**Despite these problems**, various actions can be taken to mitigate the situation.  
**The first possible measure is that** schools should organize educational programs to teach children how to use computers reponsibly. **This helps** children raise awareness about computers in education and harmful effects of spending too much time on computers. **This enables** children organize their schedules and focus only on tasks that require computer use, thereby reducing the amount of time spent on computers.  

**The second approach is that** parents should monitor both the amount of screen time their children spend on computers and the applications they use. **For example**, they can install parental control software that restricts access to certain applications and automatically locks the computer after a specific period of time. **Furthermore**, parents should also devote more time to interacting with their children and encourage outdoor activities such as camping or shopping during weekends. **Consequently**, children may reduce the amount of time spent on computers while improving their physical and mental health.  

**In conclusion, although** spending too much time on computers can give rise to many consequences, **several steps can be taken to combat the situation**.

## Giving Homework

| **Revision**: Do home assignments → have the opportunity to revise the lesson taught to them in the school → retain information more easily → improve academic performance.<br/><br/>*Làm bài tập về nhà → có cơ hội ôn lại bài học đã được dạy tại trường → giữ thông tin dễ dàng hơn → nâng cao hiệu quả học tập.* | **Excessive homework**: Return home with excessive amounts of homework → reduce children’s time for leisure & extracurricular activities → unable to develop necessary soft skills.<br/><br/>*Trở về nhà với lượng bài tập về nhà quá nhiều → giảm thời gian giải trí và hoạt động ngoại khóa của trẻ em → không thể phát triển các kỹ năng mềm cần thiết.* |


### Sample

It is true that giving homework to students has become a standard practice in modern education. Although this can bring about some benefits, it can also be detrimental to a certain extent.

On the one hand, there are several benefits of assigning home assignments to pupils. One possible advantage is that students can work independently away from the environment of schools. This encourages them to proactively apply the knowledge learnt in the classroom, thereby helping them gain a deeper understanding of the subject. Another beneficial effect could be that when they do home assignments, they have the opportunity to revise the lesson taught to them in the school. This process allows them to retain information more easily, which eventually works to improve academic performance.

Nevertheless, the fact that children are flooded with school assignments can have detrimental consequences. One issue could be that students often be assigned homework for multiple subjects. This makes them struggle to finish all assignments before deadlines, forcing them to sacrifice their sleep to meet academic demands. As a result, they suffer from exhaustion + lose interest in studying. Another drawback worth mentioning is that children frequently return home with excessive amounts of homework. This practice can reduce children’s time for leisure & extracurricular activities, thereby leaving them unable to develop necessary soft skills.

In conclusion, while the practice of giving homework can be beneficial to a certain degree, it also has several drawbacks related to students' well-being and development.
