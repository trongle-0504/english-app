# [IELTS 6.0 - Speaking - Assignment 3: Speaking Part 2]

## 🚀 Đề bài

> **Describe a favourite restaurant.**
> 
> *You should say:*
> * where it is
> * what kind of food it serves
> * how often you go there
> * and explain why you enjoy eating there.

## 📝 Bài mẫu
**(Marker giới thiệu)** There are a couple of restaurants that I can think of, but the one that comes to mind first is a Chinese restaurant called Ái Huê.

#### 1) Introduction

* **Answer:** To tell you a little bit about Ái Huê, it is located right in the city center, so it is quite convenient to get there. To give you some details, you can get there in just a few minutes from most places in the city. Now,
* **Description (What kind of food):** It mainly serves a wide variety of traditional Chinese dishes.
* **Description (How often):** Now, to tell you when I usually go there and who I go with, it is mostly on weekends or public holidays, and I typically eat there with my friends.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, it has become one of our favourite places to hang out and enjoy good food together.

#### 2) Main Body

* **Answer (Why):** Now, the reason why I enjoy eating at Ái Huê is that the food is both visually appealing and delicious.
* **Description (How / Action):** To give you some details, whenever the dishes are served, I always spend a few seconds admiring them before I start eating. because each dish is beautifully decorated with its own unique style.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, the food looks incredibly appetising, and it always makes me excited to take the first bite.
* **Effect (Impact):** Then, once I taste the food, I find that every dish is perfectly seasoned. It's neither too salty nor too bland, so it suits my taste perfectly.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, every meal feels really satisfying, and I keep coming back whenever I have the chance.

**(Conclusion)** Anyway, all in all, Ái Huê is definitely my favourite restaurant because it serves delicious food, presents every dish beautifully, and always gives me a wonderful dining experience with my friends. That's all I have to say. Thank you.

## ✍️ Bài đã được review

**(Marker giới thiệu)** There are a couple of restaurants that I can think of, but if I had to choose one, that place would definitely be **none other than** a Chinese restaurant called Ái Huê.

#### 1) Introduction

*   **Answer:** To tell you a little bit about Ái Huê, it is located right in the city center, so it is quite convenient to get there. To give you some details, you can get there in just a few minutes from most places in the city.
*   **Description (What kind of food):** It mainly serves a wide variety of traditional Chinese dishes.
*   **Description (How often):** Now, to tell you when I usually go there and who I go with, my friends and I **'ve made it a habit to** eat there **on a regular basis**, mostly on weekends or public holidays.
    *   &rarr; **Effect:** **As a result**, it has become one of our favourite places to hang out and enjoy good food together.

#### 2) Main Body

*   **Answer (Why Structure):** **What I like most about** this restaurant **is that** the food is both visually appealing and delicious.
*   **Description (How / Action):** To give you some details, whenever the dishes are served, I always spend a few seconds admiring them before I start eating, because each dish is beautifully decorated with its own unique style.
    *   &rarr; **Effect (Feeling):** **As a result**, the food looks incredibly appetising, and it always **makes me feel** excited to take the first bite.
*   **Effect (Impact / Opinion):** Then, once I taste the food, I **find it incredibly satisfying to** enjoy every dish because it is perfectly seasoned. It's neither too salty nor too bland, so it suits my taste perfectly.
    *   &rarr; **Effect (Connector):** **That's why** I keep coming back **whenever I have some time to spare**.

**(Conclusion)** Anyway, all in all, Ái Huê is definitely my favourite restaurant for the simple reason that it provides me with delicious food, presents every dish beautifully, and always gives me a wonderful dining experience with my friends. That's all I have to say. Thank you.
