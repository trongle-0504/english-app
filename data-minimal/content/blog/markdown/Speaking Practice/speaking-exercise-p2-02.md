# [IELTS 6.0 - Speaking - Assignment 3: Speaking Part 2]

## 🚀 Đề bài

> **Describe a useful skill you learned.**
> 
> *You should say:*
> * what the skill is
> * when and where you learned it
> * how you use this skill now
> * and explain why this skill is useful for you.

## 📝 Bài mẫu

**(Lead-in)** There are many useful skills that I have acquired over the years, but the one that comes to mind first is time management.

#### 1) Introduction

* **Answer:** To tell you a little bit about this skill, I first started practicing it a few years ago when I decided to pursue my master's degree.
* **Description (What & When):** This academic decision led to a situation where I had to balance a demanding job with my postgraduate studies, which put me under immense pressure.
* **Description (Where & How):** Fortunately, this stressful situation prompted me to seek help from my older brother, who is a successful project manager. He patiently showed me how to use digital planners and effectively prioritize my daily tasks based on their urgency.
* **Description (How you use it now):** Nowadays, by applying this structured method, I can easily manage my hectic routine of working full-time while studying English.

#### 2) Main Body

* **Answer (Why):** Now, the main reason why this skill is so useful for me is that it prevents me from experiencing severe burnout.
* **Description (Before):** To give you a clearer picture, before I mastered time management, I used to be a chronic procrastinator. I would often leave all my heavy assignments until the very last minute, which forced me to constantly sacrifice my sleep and perform poorly both at work and school.
* **Description (After):** However, things have changed completely since I started practicing time management. By dividing my days into specific time slots for working and learning English, I can maximize my productivity without feeling overwhelmed.
* **Effect (Linear):** This structured approach allows me to complete all my company tasks efficiently and simultaneously make rapid progress in my English skills. More importantly, it helps me maintain a healthy work-life balance, meaning I still have plenty of free time to relax and stay connected with my family.
* **Effect (Impact):** Without this skill, I would definitely fail to achieve my career and educational goals.

**(Conclusion)** Anyway, all in all, it is the most practical skill that has transformed my lifestyle. That's all I have to say. Thank you.

## ✍️ Bài đã được review

**(Lead-in)** There are quite a few valuable skills that I have picked up over the years, but if I had to choose one, it would definitely be **nothing else but** time management.

**1) Introduction**
* **Answer:** As for how I developed this skill, it all started a few years ago when I decided to embark on my master's program.
* **Description (Context):** At that time, I had to juggle a full-time job with my postgraduate studies, which pushed me to my limits and often left me feeling overwhelmed.
* **Description (Object):** Realizing that I couldn't handle everything on my own, I turned to my older brother for advice. As a successful project manager, he patiently taught me how to use digital planners, prioritize my workload, and make the most of my time.
* **Description (Habit Pattern):** Ever since then, **I've made it a habit to** plan my day structurally. Thanks to this, I can stay on top of my busy schedule while working full-time and preparing for the IELTS exam.

**2) Main Body**
* **Answer (Why Structure):** **The best part about** this skill **is that** it **helps me** avoid burning out despite having a demanding daily routine.
* **Cause / Description (Before):** To give you a better idea, before developing this habit, I was a chronic procrastinator who constantly put things off until the last minute. This gave me **no choice but to** burn the midnight oil just to meet deadlines, which **made me feel** exhausted significantly.
* **Cause / Description (After):** However, things have changed dramatically since I adopted effective time management. By setting aside dedicated blocks of time for work, studying English, and personal activities, I can make the most of every day without feeling snowed under.
* **Effect (Linear tool):** **As a result**, this habit **allows me to** meet my work deadlines with ease while making steady progress in my English. More importantly, it **gives me the opportunity to** strike a healthy work-life balance, meaning I still have plenty of time to recharge.
* **Effect (Impact):** **That's why** I can't imagine handling my daily routine without this skill because it has made a world of difference to both my career and personal life.

**(Conclusion)** All in all, time management is by far the most practical skill I have ever learned because it has completely changed the way I live and work. That's all I have to say. Thank you.
