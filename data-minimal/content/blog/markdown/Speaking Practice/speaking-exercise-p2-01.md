> * and explain why you cannot live without it.

## 📝 Bài mẫu

**(Marker giới thiệu)** There are many items that I can think of, but the one that comes to mind first is my laptop.

#### 1) Introduction

* **Answer:** To tell you a little bit about this laptop, I use it every day for studying and working.
* **Description (When):** During the daytime, I use it for my IT tasks and English learning, and at night, I use it for streaming movies.
* **Description (What it is like):** It runs really smoothly.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, I can multitask and my productivity is a lot higher.

#### 2) Main Body

* **Answer (Why):** Now, the reason why I can't live without my laptop is that it allows me to study English more effectively.
* **Description (How / action):** You know, whenever I do my homework, I make it a habit to use an online dictionary on this machine to look up new words.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, I can find information quickly and accurately, and I can finish my assignments much faster with fewer errors.
* **Effect (impact):** To give you a clearer picture, it has had a huge impact on my academic performance. Since I started using it, my exam scores have improved from around 5 or 6 to 8 or 9 points.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** That's why studying without this device would be much more challenging for me.

**(Conclusion)** Anyway, all in all, this laptop is the perfect companion for my education. That's all I have to say. Thank you.
