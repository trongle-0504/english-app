# [IELTS 6.0 - Speaking - Assignment 3: Speaking Part 2]

## 🚀 Đề bài

> **Describe a teacher who influenced you.**
> 
> *You should say:*
> * who this teacher is
> * what subject they taught
> * what was special about them
> * and explain how they have influenced you.

## 📝 Bài mẫu

**(Marker giới thiệu)** There are many teachers who have had a positive influence on my education, but the one that comes to mind first is Mr. Tho.

#### 1) Introduction

* **Answer:** To tell you a little bit about him, Mr. Tho was one of my lecturers during my master's degree.
* **Description (Who & What subject):** He taught several advanced courses related to artificial intelligence, and he also supervised my master's thesis.
* **Description (What was special):** Because I worked closely with him on my research project, I had many opportunities to learn not only from his lectures but also from his experience as a researcher.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, I gradually developed a strong interest in artificial intelligence and scientific research.

#### 2) Main Body

* **Answer (How he influenced you):** Now, the reason why Mr. Tho has had such a great influence on me is that he taught me how to conduct scientific research and think like a researcher.
* **Description (How / Action):** You know, whenever I faced difficulties during my master's thesis, he patiently guided me through every step of the research process. He showed me how to identify a meaningful research problem, read academic papers effectively, design experiments, and improve my academic writing.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, I gradually became much more confident in conducting research independently, and I was able to write academic papers much more professionally than before.
* **Effect (Impact):** More importantly, he completely changed the way I viewed artificial intelligence. Instead of treating AI as just another university subject, I began to see it as a powerful tool for solving real-world problems. This new way of thinking made me genuinely passionate about research.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** That's why I decided to pursue a long-term career in artificial intelligence, and I believe I wouldn't have chosen this path without his guidance and encouragement.

**(Conclusion)** Anyway, all in all, Mr. Tho is the teacher who has influenced me the most because he not only taught me valuable research skills but also inspired me to pursue the career that I have today. That's all I have to say. Thank you.
