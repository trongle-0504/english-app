**Part 2:**

#### Part 2:

**Subject:** Spotify (nền tảng nghe nhạc và podcast trực tuyến)

**Flow**

- **Lead-in:** Giới thiệu chủ thể (Spotify) → **Description** (mức độ phổ biến và gắn bó: cài trên cả điện thoại và máy tính).
- **Introduction (xử lý 3 cue phụ):**
  - *Cue 1 (What it is):* Ứng dụng nghe nhạc/podcast quốc tế → **Description** (đăng ký dễ dàng chỉ với email, miễn phí).
  - *Cue 2 (How & when used):* Tần suất hàng ngày nhiều lần trong ngày → **Cause** (nhạc là một phần thói quen) → **Description** (bối cảnh cụ thể: sáng chuẩn bị/đi lại, tối nấu ăn/trước khi ngủ).
  - *Cue 3 (How long used / Origin):* Dùng khoảng 5 năm trước → **Cause/Description** (bạn chia sẻ danh sách phát chung → tải về ngay vì tính cá nhân hóa cao).
- **Body (Explain how you feel about it - Cue chính):**
  - **Direct Answer:** Cảm xúc tích cực, coi ứng dụng như bản nhạc nền cuộc sống.
  - **Description:** Khả năng cá nhân hóa (thuật toán gợi ý bài hát theo gu và tâm trạng).
  - **Example:** Dự án áp lực tháng trước → **Effect** (giúp tập trung, giữ bình tĩnh và năng suất).
  - **Description (+ Positive effects):** Tính năng tiện lợi (kết nối bạn bè, tải offline, nghe podcast mở rộng kiến thức).
- **Conclusion:** Tóm tắt cảm nghĩ (nâng cao chất lượng cuộc sống thường nhật) + lời kết chuẩn.

**Sample answer**

**There are a couple of programs that I can think of, but the first one that comes to mind is** Spotify, **one of the most** popular music streaming apps out there. You know, Spotify **has become such an integral part of my life that** I have it installed on both my phone and laptop.

**To tell you a little bit about what it is, it’s a program that** lets you listen to millions of songs, podcasts, and playlists from artists all over the world. **All you need is** an email to sign up for an account and you're good to go, no subscription fee required. As for when and how I use it, **pretty much every single day**, **sometimes multiple times a day, because** music is such a big part of my routine. I usually open it **in the morning while** getting ready or during my commute, **and again in the evening when** I’m cooking or winding down before bed. **If my memory serves me right, it was about** five years ago. A friend was sharing a collaborative playlist with me, and I downloaded it right away after seeing how easy and personalized it was.

**Now, to tell you how I feel about it, I'm really** positive about Spotify. **It’s become almost like** a personal soundtrack to my life, you know? The way it learns my taste and creates those perfect “Discover Weekly” playlists **never stops amazing me**; it often introduces me to new artists that perfectly match my mood. **For example,** during a particularly stressful project last month, I relied on its focus playlists to **keep me** productive, and it genuinely **helped me** stay calm and concentrated. **I love how it** blends technology with creativity, **making it easy to** share music with friends or even attend virtual concerts. **It’s incredibly convenient**, with offline downloads that save me when I’m traveling or have poor internet. Plus, the podcasts section has broadened my knowledge on so many topics. **Overall,** Spotify just **makes everyday moments** more enjoyable and reminds me that little things like the right song can turn an ordinary day into something special.

##### CÂU HỎI 1: What kinds of programs or apps are popular in your country?

**Question type:** What kind of
**Answer shape:** C (Specify) — Câu hỏi đề cập tới danh từ chung ("programs or apps"), vì vậy phân tách theo đối tượng người dùng (young people vs. older people) giúp câu trả lời có chiều sâu và không bị khái quát hóa quá đà.
**Direct-answer structure used:** `It totally depends on [NC] really` & `It would be …`

**Flow**

- ***Marker 1*** (depends on generation)
- ***Marker 2*** (young people) → ***Answer*** (social media & short-video apps)
- ***Cause*** (need connection/entertainment)
- ***Effect*** (spend hours daily)
- ***Marker 2*** (older generation) → ***Answer*** (messaging & news apps)
- ***Cause*** (keep in touch & read daily updates)

**Sample answer**

**It totally depends on the group of people we're talking about, really.** **When it comes to** young people, **it would be** social media and short-video platforms like TikTok and Instagram. **This is because** these apps **allow them to** kill time easily and stay connected with their peer group. **As a result**, many teenagers and students **make it a habit to** scroll through these feeds on a daily basis. **Now, if we're talking about** the older generation, **it would be** messaging platforms like Zalo or news applications. **The simple reason for this is that** they mainly **need to** keep in touch with family members and read local news, rather than consuming short-form viral videos.

##### CÂU HỎI 2. Why do some people not like using apps?

**Flow**

- ***Answer*** (avoid apps due to complexity/utility)
- ***Description / Cause*** (privacy & data breach worries)
- ***Description / Preference*** (prefer traditional ways over digital tools)
- ***Example*** (middle-aged & older Vietnamese people choosing cash and direct contact)
- ***Cause / Negative effect*** (addiction & constant notifications)
- ***Effect / Conclusion*** (root causes: habit, trust, simplicity)

**Sample answer**

To be honest, many people, especially older generations, avoid using apps because they **find them complicated** or unnecessary. Some worry about privacy and data security, as there have been many stories of information leaks. Others simply **prefer traditional ways**: making phone calls **instead of** messaging or shopping in physical stores. **In Vietnam**, many middle-aged and older people **still feel more comfortable with** cash and direct human interaction rather than digital transactions. Additionally, some people **don’t like** the addictive nature of apps and the constant notifications. I reckon it often comes down to habit, trust issues, and a desire for simplicity.

**It would be a couple of things, one of which is** the core purpose behind why they go online. **When it comes to** the younger crowd, they **are so into** dynamic platforms like TikTok, Instagram, or mobile gaming. **This is because** they **truly long to** express their lifestyle, produce creative content, and stay constantly entertained. **As a result**, they **make it a habit to** switch between multiple applications fluently throughout the day. **On the other hand**, older people **tend to prefer** practical utilities **to** entertainment apps. **A big part of the reason goes to** the fact that they only **need to** handle basic necessities—such as messaging on Zalo, reading daily news, or checking digital banking. **That's why** they **consider apps to be** purely functional tools rather than lifestyle accessories.

##### CÂU HỎI 4: Is it necessary for parents to limit the time their children spend on apps or computer programs?

**Question type:** Yes/No
**Answer shape:** D (Two-sided) — Trả lời khẳng định việc thiết lập giới hạn là cần thiết, nêu nguyên nhân tác hại, sau đó chỉ ra mặt trái của việc cấm đoán hoàn toàn (`That said`) và đề xuất giải pháp đặt ra ranh giới hợp lý.
**Direct-answer structure used:** `Without a doubt, I would say so.` (§1.1)

**Flow**

- ***Direct Answer*** (Yes, necessary to set limits)
- ***Cause*** (excessive screen time harms health & study time)
- ***Effect*** (kids find it hard to build real-life social skills)
- ***Marker***: `That said` (a total ban is unrealistic & causes rebellion)
- ***Example / Solution*** (Vietnamese families using "homework first" rule & parental control apps)
- ***Effect / Conclusion*** (consistent, fair limits beat strict prohibition in fostering self-discipline)

**Sample answer**

**Without a doubt, I would say so.** **This is because** unrestricted screen time can harm a child's eyesight, sleep quality, and physical health, while also **preventing them from** spending time on academic work. **As a result**, kids who are glued to screens all day often **find it challenging to** build real-life social skills. **That said**, a total ban is **not exactly very** realistic, as it can easily **make children** rebel. **Take many families in Vietnam as an example**; parents usually **make a habit out of** applying a "homework first, then play" rule alongside parental control apps to set bedtime curfews. **That's why** maintaining fair, consistent boundaries **totally beats** strict prohibition **in terms of** fostering long-term self-discipline.

##### CÂU HỎI 5: What are the benefits of using apps as a tool for education?

##### CÂU HỎI 6: How have mobile apps changed people’s daily habits?

##### CÂU HỎI 7: Are there any downsides to using apps too much?

**Question type:** Yes/No & Disadvantages
**Answer shape:** A (Straight chain) — Trả lời khẳng định tác hại của việc lạm dụng ứng dụng, sau đó phân tích chuỗi nguyên nhân - hậu quả trên 3 phương diện: nghiện màn hình (screen addiction), rủi ro bảo mật (privacy risks), và áp lực tâm lý (mental stress).

**For sure, I would say so.** **The worst part about overusing apps is that** it can easily lead to screen addiction, **preventing people from** spending time on real-life interactions and deep work. **A big part of the concern also goes to** data privacy, as many apps track personal information without users fully realizing what they are sharing. Furthermore, the pressure of being "always connected" can **make users feel** constantly stressed to reply instantly or compare themselves to others online. **That's why** I believe it is essential to use digital tools with clear intention; otherwise, they will end up running our lives.

In fact, many great movies succeed with unknown actors **for the simple reason that** they have strong storytelling. **That's why** I believe directors **should** focus on finding the right fit for the role **rather than** just paying for fame.
