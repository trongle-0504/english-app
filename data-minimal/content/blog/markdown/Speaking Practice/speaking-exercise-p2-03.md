# [IELTS 6.0 - Speaking - Assignment 3: Speaking Part 2]

> **Describe an important friend.**
> 
> *You should say:*
> * who this friend is

> * and explain why this friend is important to you.

## 📝 Bài mẫu

**(Marker giới thiệu)** There are many people who have played an important role in my life, but the one that comes to mind first is my close friend, Vu.

#### 1) Introduction

* **Answer:** To tell you a little bit about him, Vu has been one of my closest friends since secondary school.
* **Description (How you met):** We first met several years ago when I transferred to a new school near my house, and our homeroom teacher happened to arrange for us to sit next to each other.
* **Description (What you usually do together):** Since then, we have spent a lot of time studying together, attending extra classes, and sometimes playing sports after school.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, we gradually became close friends and always supported each other both in school and in everyday life.

#### 2) Main Body

* **Answer (Why):** Now, the reason why Vu is so important to me is that he helped me become a more confident student and a better person.
* **Description (How / Action):** You know, whenever I had difficulty understanding a lesson, he was always willing to explain it to me patiently. Besides that, he often encouraged me to join school activities and group projects instead of staying quiet by myself.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** As a result, I gradually became more confident when communicating with other classmates, and I also found studying much more enjoyable.
* **Effect (Impact):** To give you a clearer picture, my academic performance improved significantly. My exam scores increased from around 5 or 6 to about 8 or 9, and I became much more active in class than before.
  * &rarr; **Effect:** That's why I believe Vu has had a huge influence on both my academic success and my personal growth, and I honestly can't imagine what my school life would have been like without his support.

**(Conclusion)** Anyway, all in all, Vu is much more than just a close friend. He has always supported me during difficult times, helped me improve both academically and personally, and that is why he is such an important person in my life. That's all I have to say. Thank you.

## ✍️ Bài đã được review


**(Marker giới thiệu)** There are many people who have played an important role in my life, but if I had to choose, that person would definitely be **no one else but** my close friend, Vu.

#### 1) Introduction

* **Answer:** To tell you a little bit about him, Vu has been one of my closest friends since secondary school.
* **Description (How you met):** **If I remember correctly, it was** a long time ago when I transferred to a new school, and our homeroom teacher happened to assign us to be desk mates. That's how we first got to know each other and gradually became friends.
* **Description (What you usually do together):** Since then, we **'ve made it a habit to** spend a great deal of time hitting the books together, attending extra classes, and sometimes playing sports after school.
* **Effect:** **As a result**, we gradually struck up a strong friendship and have always stood by each other through thick and thin, both at school and in everyday life.

#### 2) Main Body

* **Answer (Why Structure):** **A big part of the reason goes to the fact that** he helped me become a more confident student and a better person.
* **Description (How / Action):** You know, whenever I struggled to understand certain lessons, he was always willing to walk me through difficult concepts until I fully understood them. Besides that, he often **encouraged me to** take part in school activities and group projects instead of keeping to myself.
* **Effect (Feeling):** Thanks to his encouragement, I gradually came out of my shell, which **made me feel** much more confident when communicating with my classmates. I also started to find studying much more enjoyable than before.
* **Effect (Impact):** Looking back, I can clearly see a significant improvement in my academic performance. My grades shot up from around 5 or 6 to about 8 or 9, and I became much more active in class.
* **Effect (Connector):** **That's why** I believe Vu has exerted a significant influence on both my academic success and personal growth. I honestly can't imagine what my school life would have been like without his support.

**(Conclusion)** Anyway, all in all, Vu is much more than just a close friend. He has always stood by me during difficult times, **encouraged me to** become a better version of myself, and **that is why** he holds a special place in my heart. That's all I have to say. Thank you.

## 📚 Vocabulary

Dựa trên bài IELTS Speaking Part 2 của bạn, mình thấy bạn đang xây dựng theo hướng **kể chuyện + dùng collocation tự nhiên**, đây là hướng tốt để lên 6.5–7.0. Mình sẽ tách ra thành:

1. **Collocations nên học**
2. **Verb patterns (cấu trúc động từ) nên học**
3. **Mẫu câu có thể tái sử dụng cho nhiều chủ đề IELTS**

<div class="text-lg font-bold text-emerald-600 dark:text-emerald-400 mb-4 mt-8">2. Verb patterns quan trọng trong IELTS Speaking</div>

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2">1. Help + someone + (to) V (Cực kỳ phổ biến)</div>

* He helped me **become** more confident.
* My teacher helped me **improve** my English.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">2. Encourage + someone + to V</div>

* He encouraged me **to participate** in activities.
* My parents encouraged me **to pursue** my dream.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">3. Assign + someone + to V / noun</div>

* The teacher assigned us **to sit together**.
* I was assigned **to a new project**.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">4. Allow + someone + to V</div>

* My friend allowed me **to express** my opinions.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">5. Enable + someone + to V (trang trọng hơn help)</div>

* This experience enabled me **to develop** confidence.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">6. Teach + someone + how to V</div>

* He taught me how to study effectively.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">7. Remind + someone + to V</div>

* My teacher reminded me to stay focused.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">8. Inspire + someone + to V</div>

* My professor inspired me to pursue AI research.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">9. Motivate + someone + to V</div>

* My friend motivated me to work harder.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">10. Prevent + someone + from + V-ing</div>

* He prevented me from giving up.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">11. Stop + someone + from + V-ing</div>

* My parents stopped me from making the wrong decision.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">12. Spend time + V-ing</div>

We spent a great deal of time **studying together**.

*Biến thể:*
* spend time doing something
* spend money doing something

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">13. Make + someone + adjective (Rất quan trọng trong Speaking)</div>

* It made me **feel more confident**.
* The experience made me **more independent**.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">14. Find + something + adjective</div>

* I found the experience extremely rewarding.
* I found studying with him enjoyable.

<div class="text-base font-semibold text-slate-800 dark:text-slate-200 mb-2 mt-4">15. Consider + V-ing</div>

* I consider learning English essential.
