## 📝 Bài mẫu

There are several parents who **opt to** provide their children with **home education** rather than formal schooling. Although this practice has several advantages, the drawbacks may be far more significant.

On the one hand, there can be some minor benefits of children being taught at home. One advantage could be that homeschooling can be a suitable choice for children with **mental illnesses** such as autism. This is because these students may not **cope well with stress** resulting from schoolwork and **peer pressure**, which means that they can avoid stress and feel more comfortable when being taught at home. Another advantage worth mentioning is that **academically-deficient children** who need more time to learn compared to their peers can learn **at their own pace**. The reason for this is that parents can spend more time working with their children, thereby helping them understand and **master a material before advancing to other subjects**.
