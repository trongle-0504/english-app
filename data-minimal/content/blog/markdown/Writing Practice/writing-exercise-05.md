# WT2 - Advantage & Disadvantage - Đề 3 - Education

## 🚀 Đề bài

> In many countries, some students live with their families while studying, but other students prefer to attend universities in other cities. 
> Do the advantages of living away from home outweigh the disadvantages?

---

## 🧠 Dàn ý

<div class="my-6 bg-slate-50 dark:bg-slate-900/40 border border-slate-200 dark:border-slate-800 rounded-2xl p-6">

**Body paragraph 1: Advantages of living away from home (Independent living skills)**

**Cultivate independent living skills**

* Move away from home >>> no longer receive direct support from families on a daily basis >>> have to take full responsibility for various aspects of their lives (managing accommodation, cooking, scheduling).
* Ex: Learn how to budget expenses wisely >>> avoid running out of money before the end of the month >>> have a stronger foundation for becoming independent adults than relying on parents.

**Body paragraph 2: Other advantages (Academic & Networking)**

**Better academic environments and wider networking opportunities**

* Major cities host prestigious universities with superior facilities >>> relocating students have more time and resources to focus on their studies.
* Ex: Live in a new environment close to their peers >>> discuss assignments and participate in extracurricular activities together >>> get substantial academic benefits and build diverse social connections.

</div>

<u>It is sometimes believed that</u> university students <u>are likely to benefit more from</u> *moving to other cities for their studies* <u>than from</u> *staying with their families*. **In my opinion**, the *advantages of living away from home* **significantly outweigh** the *disadvantages* for several reasons.

**The first argument given to support my view is that** *living away from home* in a different city <u>helps students cultivate</u> *independent living skills*. **This is because** students who relocate no longer receive *direct support* from their families *on a daily basis*. **Consequently**, they have to <u>take full responsibility for</u> various *aspects of their lives*, such as *managing their accommodation*, cooking, and *organizing their personal schedules*. **For instance**, many university students living in a new city learn how to *budget their expenses wisely*, **<u>allowing them to</u>** <u>avoid</u> *running out of money* before the end of the month. **This** experience <u>provides them with a stronger foundation for</u> *becoming independent adults* <u>than</u> *relying on* their parents at home.

**Another point in support of my belief is that** *attending universities in other cities* offers students *better academic environments* and *wider networking opportunities*. Major cities often host *prestigious institutions* with *superior facilities*, **<u>which</u>** <u>gives relocating students</u> more time and resources to focus on their studies. **Furthermore**, living in a new environment *close to their peers* creates *valuable opportunities* for students to *discuss assignments* and *participate in extracurricular activities* together. **For these reasons**, **I believe that** *moving to another city* for *higher education* <u>provides students with</u> *substantial academic benefits* and *rich opportunities* to <u>build diverse social connections</u>.

**In conclusion**, **I firmly believe that** the *advantages of living away from home* while attending university, **<u>namely</u>** *fostering independence* and *accessing better academic and social opportunities*, **far outweigh** the *disadvantages*.
