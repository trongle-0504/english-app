# WT2 - Agree or Disagree - Đề 1 - Education

## 🚀 Đề bài

> It is better for college students to live in schools than live at home with their parents. Do you agree or disagree?

---

### **Mở bài (Introduction)**
* **Topic:** Living in school (dormitories) vs living at home with parents.
* **Thesis:** Totally agree (hoàn toàn đồng ý với việc sống ở trường/ký túc xá có lợi hơn).

### **Thân bài 1 (Body 1): Cultivate independent living skills**
* **Lý do:** Không nhận được hỗ trợ trực tiếp hàng ngày từ bố mẹ &rarr; phải tự lập.
* **Hành động:** Tự chịu trách nhiệm cho các công việc hàng ngày (nấu ăn, sắp xếp lịch trình).
* **Ví dụ:** Học sinh năm nhất học cách quản lý chi tiêu &rarr; tránh hết tiền cuối tháng.
* **Kết luận:** Tạo nền tảng tốt để trở thành người lớn tự lập.

### **Thân bài 2 (Body 2): Better academic environment & networking opportunities**
* **Lý do 1 (Học tập):** Tiết kiệm thời gian di chuyển (commuting time) &rarr; có nhiều thời gian tự học & sử dụng cơ sở vật chất (thư viện).
* **Lý do 2 (Mạng lưới quan hệ):** Sống gần bạn bè cùng trang lứa &rarr; dễ dàng thảo luận bài tập, tham gia hoạt động ngoại khóa &rarr; xây dựng mối quan hệ xã hội.

### **Kết bài (Conclusion)**
* Khẳng định lại quan điểm: Sống trên campus mang lại nhiều lợi ích tự lập và học tập hơn sống cùng bố mẹ.

## 📝 Bài mẫu

It is sometimes believed that college students are likely to benefit more from living in school than from staying with their parents. This essay totally agrees with this suggestion for several reasons.

The first argument given to support my opinion is that living in school dormitories helps students cultivate independent living skills. This is because students who live away from home no longer receive direct support from their parents on a daily basis. Consequently, they have to take responsibility for various aspects of their lives such as cooking and organizing their personal schedules. For instance, many first-year students in dormitories learn how to manage their expenses wisely, allowing them to avoid running out of money at the end of the month. This is why I think that this experience provides them with a better foundation for becoming independent adults than continuing to rely on their parents at home.

Another point behind my belief is that living in school dormitories offers students better academic environments and networking opportunities. This is because living in a dormitory helps students save commuting time, thereby allowing them to have more time to study and use campus facilities such as libraries. In fact, living close to their peers creates valuable opportunities for students to discuss assignments and participate in extracurricular activities together. For these reasons, I believe that living on campus provides students with significant academic benefits and opportunities to build social connections compared with living at home.

In conclusion, I totally agree that it is more beneficial for college students to live on campus than to live at home with their parents, given the aforementioned arguments.
