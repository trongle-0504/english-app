# WT2 - Causes & Solutions - Đề 1 - Health

## 🚀 Đề bài

> More and more people today are drinking sugar-based drinks. What are the reasons for this? What are the solutions to make people drink less?

---

## 🧠 Dàn ý

<div class="my-6 bg-slate-50 dark:bg-slate-900/40 border border-slate-200 dark:border-slate-800 rounded-2xl p-6">

### **Body paragraph 1: Reasons**

* **Soft-drink companies heavily promote products**
  * Soft-drink companies invest in massive advertising campaigns &rarr; people constantly exposed and tempted to buy on a daily basis.
* **Modern lifestyle contributes to this trend**
  * Busy people often face hectic work schedules &rarr; lack time to prepare healthy meals &rarr; choose convenient ready-to-drink sugary beverages (e.g. office workers exhausted and rely on energy drinks).

### **Body paragraph 2: Solutions**

* **Government regulations**
  * Impose stricter regulations on advertising &rarr; ban commercials during children's prime-time TV shows &rarr; less exposed and feel less tempted to buy.
* **Workplace promotion**
  * Workplaces promote healthier eating habits & time management &rarr; stock office pantries with healthy snacks and drinks &rarr; choose nutritious options during hectic working hours.

</div>

<u>It is becoming more common for</u> people to *consume sugar-based drinks* nowadays. **This** <u>can be attributed to</u> *several factors*, <u>which</u> *require possible solutions to be adopted*.

**On the one hand**, **there are several explanations as to why** *the consumption of sugary drinks* is rising nowadays. **Perhaps one of these is that** *soft-drink companies* *heavily promote their products*, <u>which</u> directly increases the consumption of these beverages. **In fact**, these companies *invest in massive advertising campaigns* to *attract the attention of potential consumers*. **As a result**, people are <u>constantly exposed to</u> these advertisements and <u>tempted to buy</u> these sugary beverages *on a daily basis*. **Another point worth mentioning is that** the *modern lifestyle* contributes to this trend. Busy people often face *hectic work schedules* and *lack the time to prepare* healthy meals at home. **This** <u>leads them to choose</u> convenient, and *ready-to-drink sugary beverages*. **For example**, office workers often feel exhausted due to their *heavy workload*. **Consequently**, they *rely on energy drinks* to *boost their productivity*.

**Various measures, nevertheless, can be** <u>implemented to</u> address these issues. **The first possible measure is that** the government should *impose stricter regulations on* the advertising of sugary drinks, <u>which</u> reduces the overall consumption of these *unhealthy beverages*. **Specifically**, authorities can ban *soft-drink commercials* during children's *prime-time TV shows*, **<u>thereby</u>** <u>ensuring that</u> people are *less exposed to* these advertisements and *feel less tempted to buy* them. **The second approach is that** workplaces should promote *healthier eating habits* and *time management*. **This** encourages people to choose *healthier alternatives* over sugary drinks. **For instance**, companies can *stock office pantries* with healthy snacks and drinks. **This** <u>allows people to choose</u> *nutritious options* instantly even during their most *hectic working hours*.

**In conclusion**, <u>while</u> <u>many reasons can be given to explain why</u> *the consumption of sugary drinks* is rising, **several steps can be** <u>taken to</u> *combat the situation*.
