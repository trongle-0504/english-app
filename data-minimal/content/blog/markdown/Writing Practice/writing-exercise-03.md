# WT2 - Advantage & Disadvantage - Đề 2 - Technology

## 🚀 Đề bài

> The use of social media, such as Facebook and Twitter, is replacing face-to-face contact in everyday life. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages of this development?

---

## 🧠 Dàn ý

<div class="my-6 bg-slate-50 dark:bg-slate-900/40 border border-slate-200 dark:border-slate-800 rounded-2xl p-6">

### **Body paragraph 1: Disadvantages**

* **Overusing social media instead of real-life activities**
  * Cause addiction &rarr; spend too much time surfing internet / staring at screens &rarr; discourage from outdoor activities &rarr; rise obesity rates.
* **Seek connections on social media can bring severe privacy risks**
  * Dangerous exposure of too much personal information (e.g. reveal sensitive details about daily lives to attract virtual friends/partners) &rarr; exploit by people with bad motives.

### **Body paragraph 2: Advantages**

* **Connect instantly regardless of geographical distance**
  * Overcome physical barriers in daily communication &rarr; send messages/video calls globally within seconds &rarr; stay constantly updated and closely in touch with faraway friends.
* **Share socially significant posts easily**
  * Deliver messages about important social issues to a larger audience &rarr; share digital content about environmental pollution, teenage behaviors &rarr; raise public awareness &rarr; promote collective efforts to address global problems.

</div>

**It is true that** *face-to-face interaction* is increasingly <u>being replaced by</u> *the use of social media* in everyday life. <u>Although</u> **this** has several *disadvantages*, **the benefits may be far more significant**.

**On the one hand**, there can be some *minor disadvantages* of replacing *face-to-face interaction*. **One possible issue is that** *overusing social media* instead of *engaging in real-life activities* can easily *cause addiction*, **<u>thereby</u>** <u>forcing individuals to spend</u> too much time *surfing the internet*. **For instance**, many teenagers often spend their entire weekends <u>staring at screens</u>. **Consequently**, **this habit** discourages them from *outdoor activities* and <u>results in rising obesity rates</u>. **Another detrimental effect could be that** using social media as *the primary tool to seek connections* can <u>bring severe privacy risks</u>. **This** <u>can lead to the dangerous exposure of</u> too much *personal information*. **In fact**, to *attract virtual friends*, romantic partners, users often <u>reveal sensitive details</u> about their daily lives. **This** <u>allows people with bad motives to exploit</u> this information.

**Nevertheless**, replacing *face-to-face interaction* can have *major advantages* <u>which</u> *overshadow* these *negative impacts*. **The first benefit is that** social media <u>allows people to connect instantly</u> *regardless of the geographical distance*, <u>which</u> <u>enables them to</u> *overcome physical barriers* in *daily communication*. **As a result**, users <u>can send messages, make video call globally within seconds</u>, **and thus** people can stay <u>constantly updated about</u> others' lives and *stay closely in touch* with *faraway friends*. **The second merit worth mentioning is that** social media <u>allows people to share</u> *socially significant posts* easily, <u>which</u> *delivers messages about* *important social issues* to a *larger audience*. **As a result**, people can *create and share digital content* about *environmental pollution*, *teenage behaviors*, **and <u>thereby</u>** <u>raising public awareness</u> and <u>promoting collective efforts to address global problems</u>.

**In conclusion**, <u>despite</u> the *potential downsides* of replacing *face-to-face interaction*, **the beneficial effects brought about are of greater importance**.
