### A. NÊU QUAN ĐIỂM & GIỚI THIỆU LUẬN ĐIỂM


<a id="nhom-a1"></a>
#### A1. Nêu quan điểm cá nhân — *neo: "I believe"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">(I would) argue / contend / maintain that S + V</span> | tôi cho rằng / tôi vẫn giữ quan điểm rằng | **I would argue that** governments should invest far more heavily in renewable energy infrastructure. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I am of the opinion that S + V</span> | tôi cho rằng | **I am of the opinion that** university tuition should remain free for all citizens. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I hold the belief that S + V</span> | tôi tin rằng | **I hold the belief that** early exposure to foreign languages benefits children enormously. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I firmly believe that S + V</span> | tôi tin chắc rằng | **I firmly believe that** stricter emission standards are necessary to combat urban air pollution. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I am (highly) convinced that S + V</span> | tôi hoàn toàn tin rằng | **I am highly convinced that** remote working arrangements improve both productivity and employee wellbeing. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">my contention is that S + V</span> | quan điểm của tôi là | **My contention is that** museums should charge no entrance fee to local residents. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">from my perspective</span> | theo quan điểm của tôi | **From my perspective**, public transport subsidies represent the most effective solution to congestion. |

<a id="nhom-a2"></a>
#### A2. Bày tỏ ủng hộ / đồng tình — *neo: "agree with"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I am in favour of + N</span> | tôi ủng hộ / nghiêng về | **I am in favour of** compulsory science education throughout the entire secondary school curriculum. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I largely agree that S + V</span> | tôi phần lớn đồng tình rằng | **I largely agree that** social media platforms should be held responsible for misinformation. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I wholeheartedly agree with this assertion</span> | tôi hoàn toàn tán thành nhận định này | Given the evidence on childhood obesity, **I wholeheartedly agree with this assertion** about advertising. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">advocate for + N</span> | ủng hộ / cổ vũ cho | Many economists **advocate for** higher carbon taxes as a means of reducing emissions. |

<a id="nhom-a3"></a>
#### A3. Bày tỏ phản đối — *neo: "disagree with"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I strongly disagree with the idea / notion that S + V</span> | tôi hoàn toàn không đồng tình với ý kiến / quan niệm rằng | **I strongly disagree with the idea that** technology has made traditional classroom teaching redundant. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I firmly disagree with this proposal</span> | tôi kiên quyết phản đối đề xuất này | Because rural communities would suffer, **I firmly disagree with this proposal** to close local hospitals. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I cannot concur that S + V</span> | tôi không thể đồng tình rằng | **I cannot concur that** economic growth should always take precedence over environmental protection. |

<a id="nhom-a4"></a>
#### A4. Trích dẫn quan điểm chung / người khác — *neo: "it is believed that"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">it is sometimes / often believed / contended that S + V</span> | đôi khi / thường thì người ta cho rằng | **It is often contended that** modern parents spend insufficient time with their children. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">it is often proposed that S + V</span> | người ta thường đề xuất rằng | **It is often proposed that** governments should tax sugary drinks to improve public health. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">it is a commonly held belief that S + V</span> | nhiều người vẫn tin rằng | **It is a commonly held belief that** violent video games encourage aggressive adolescent behaviour. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">the assertion that S + V</span> | nhận định cho rằng | **The assertion that** automation will eliminate most jobs deserves considerably closer scrutiny. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">proponents of sth argue that S + V</span> | những người ủng hộ lập luận rằng | **Proponents of nuclear power argue that** it offers reliable low-carbon electricity for growing cities. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">they may well argue that S + V</span> | họ có thể lập luận rằng | **They may well argue that** private healthcare shortens waiting times for ordinary patients. |

<a id="nhom-a5"></a>
#### A5. Nêu tranh luận, ý kiến trái chiều — *neo: "opinions are divided"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">opinions are divided on whether S + V</span> | ý kiến trái chiều về việc liệu | **Opinions are divided on whether** schools should replace printed textbooks with digital tablets. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">there is ongoing debate over whether S + V</span> | vẫn còn tranh luận về việc liệu | **There is ongoing debate over whether** governments should censor harmful content on social media. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">spark debate over whether S + V</span> | làm dấy lên tranh luận về việc liệu | Driverless vehicles have **sparked debate over whether** existing traffic laws remain adequate today. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">remain widely debated</span> | vẫn còn nhiều tranh luận | The benefits of homework for primary pupils **remain widely debated** among education researchers. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be a subject of considerable societal discussion</span> | là đề tài được xã hội bàn luận nhiều | Genetic engineering **is a subject of considerable societal discussion** in many developed countries today. |

### B. NHƯỢNG BỘ & PHẢN BIỆN


<a id="nhom-b1"></a>
#### B1. Thừa nhận điểm đúng của phía đối lập — *neo: "acknowledge that"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">it must be acknowledged that S + V</span> | phải thừa nhận rằng | **It must be acknowledged that** private schools often achieve superior examination results. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">I acknowledge that S + V</span> | tôi thừa nhận rằng | **I acknowledge that** tourism generates substantial revenue for many developing coastal economies. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">while acknowledging + N</span> | dù thừa nhận rằng | **While acknowledging** the convenience of online shopping, I remain concerned about local retailers. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">while it is true that S + V</span> | dù đúng là | **While it is true that** cars pollute heavily, banning them entirely seems impractical. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">it is undeniable that S + V</span> | không thể phủ nhận rằng | **It is undeniable that** the internet has transformed how students access academic information. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">despite these potential merits</span> | dù có những ưu điểm tiềm năng đó | **Despite these potential merits**, nuclear energy still poses serious waste-disposal problems for governments. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">have significant merit</span> | có giá trị đáng kể | The argument for four-day working weeks **has significant merit** in reducing employee burnout. |

<a id="nhom-b2"></a>
#### B2. Chỉ ra giới hạn của lập luận đối lập — *neo: "does not always hold true"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">this argument does not always hold true</span> | lập luận này không luôn đúng | In rural regions with poor connectivity, **this argument does not always hold true**. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">overlook the fact that S + V</span> | bỏ qua thực tế rằng | Such critics **overlook the fact that** immigrants fill critical shortages in healthcare professions. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be far from universal</span> | không hề đúng với tất cả | Access to clean drinking water **is far from universal** across sub-Saharan African communities. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">cannot be dismissed as + N</span> | không thể bị gạt đi như là | Public anxiety about artificial intelligence **cannot be dismissed as** irrational technophobia by policymakers. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">cannot be overlooked / underestimated</span> | không thể bỏ qua / không thể xem nhẹ | The psychological impact of long commutes on workers **cannot be underestimated** by employers. |

<a id="nhom-b3"></a>
#### B3. Luận điểm chưa đủ nhưng vẫn khả thi — *neo: "insufficient... not insurmountable"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be an insufficient solution on its own / not a standalone solution</span> | bản thân nó chưa đủ để giải quyết | Recycling **is an insufficient solution on its own** to the global plastic waste crisis. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be insufficient to justify + N</span> | không đủ để biện minh cho | Short-term economic gains **are insufficient to justify** the destruction of ancient rainforest habitats. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be partly undermined by + N</span> | bị giảm hiệu quả một phần bởi | This advantage **is partly undermined by** the considerable cost of maintaining such digital infrastructure. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">Problematic as it is, S + V</span> | dù vấn đề khá nan giải, nhưng | **Problematic as it is**, mass tourism remains an essential income source for coastal towns. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be not insurmountable</span> | không phải là không thể vượt qua | These technical obstacles to renewable storage **are not insurmountable** given sufficient research funding. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">present serious concerns</span> | đặt ra những lo ngại nghiêm trọng | Facial recognition cameras in public spaces **present serious concerns** regarding individual privacy rights. |

### C. NGUYÊN NHÂN


<a id="nhom-c1"></a>
#### C1. Nguồn gốc trực tiếp — *neo: "stem from"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">stem (directly) from + N</span> | bắt nguồn (trực tiếp) từ | Many childhood health problems **stem directly from** poor dietary habits established at home. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">arise from + N</span> | nảy sinh từ | Traffic congestion in major cities largely **arises from** inadequate investment in public transport. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be (deeply) rooted in + N</span> | có gốc rễ / bắt nguồn sâu xa từ | Gender inequality in the workplace **is deeply rooted in** long-standing cultural expectations about caregiving. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be rooted in the belief that S + V</span> | xuất phát từ niềm tin rằng | This policy **is rooted in the belief that** competition inevitably raises educational standards. |

<a id="nhom-c2"></a>
#### C2. Nguyên nhân được quy cho / thúc đẩy bởi — *neo: "be attributed to"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be attributed / attributable / ascribed to + N</span> | được cho là do / được quy cho là do | The recent decline in youth crime **can be attributed to** improved community sports programmes. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be driven by + N</span> | được thúc đẩy bởi | Rapid urbanisation in developing nations **is driven by** the promise of better employment. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be accelerated by + N</span> | bị đẩy nhanh bởi | Coastal erosion **has been accelerated by** rising sea levels and increasingly frequent storms. |

<a id="nhom-c3"></a>
#### C3. Danh từ chỉ nguyên nhân chính — *neo: "root causes"*

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">the root / leading / principal causes (of + N)</span> | nguyên nhân gốc rễ / hàng đầu / chủ yếu (của) | Poverty and unemployment remain **the root causes of** rising crime in deprived urban neighbourhoods. |
