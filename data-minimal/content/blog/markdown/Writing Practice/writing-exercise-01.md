# [IELTS 6.0 - Sample WT2 - Advantage & disadvantage - Đề 1] - Technology

## 🚀 Đề bài

> [New technologies have changed the way children spend their free time. Do advantages of this outweigh disadvantages?]

---

## 🧠 Dàn ý

<div class="my-6 bg-slate-50 dark:bg-slate-900/40 border border-slate-200 dark:border-slate-800 rounded-2xl p-6">

### **Body paragraph 1:**

#### **Drawbacks**

* **Children are too young**
* Do not know how to behave on social media platforms >>> give rise to some negative consequences.
* Ex: Make inappropriate comments in Facebook posts >>> be criticized or attacked >>> feel threatened and anxious.


* **Easily affected by advertisements of unhealthy products**
* Ex: See advertisements from fast food companies >>> nag their parents into buying them these food items on a regular basis >>> increase the risk of childhood obesity/

### **Body paragraph 2:**

#### **Benefits**

* **Enrich the life of children**
* Gain access to entertainment content from different religious and cultural backgrounds >>> have a better understanding of cultural differences >>> become more open-minded


* **Broaden children's knowledge >>> Watching educational programs on television**
* Ex: Form a particular interest in shows about nature channels >>> learn more about wildlife >>> become more aware about the importance of protecting the environment

**It is true that** the way children spend their *free time* has been <u>changed by</u> *modern technologies*. <u>Although</u> **this development** has several *disadvantages*, **the benefits are far more significant, in my opinion**.

**On the one hand**, there can be some *minor drawbacks* of children <u>using</u> *technologies* during their *leisure time*. **One possible disadvantage could be that** children are too young and **thus** may not know how to *behave appropriately* on *social media platforms*, **<u>thereby</u>** *giving rise to* some *negative consequences*. **For example**, some children may *make inappropriate comments* in a Facebook post; **consequently**, they <u>can be criticized or even attacked by</u> other users, <u>which</u> can make them feel *threatened and anxious*. **Another downside worth mentioning is that** several young children now watch televisions *on a daily basis*, <u>meaning that</u> they <u>can be easily affected by</u> *advertisements of unhealthy products*. **For instance**, <u>when</u> some children see advertisements from fast food companies **such as** McDonald’s, they can *nag* their parents <u>into buying</u> them these food items *on a regular basis*, <u>which</u> can *increase the risk of* *childhood obesity*.

**Nevertheless**, <u>the fact that</u> children spend their *free time* <u>using</u> *technologies* can have several *major benefits* <u>which</u> *overshadow* these *negative impacts*. **One beneficial effect is that** the use of technology can *enrich the life* of children. **This is because** children <u>who</u> start using *social media platforms* **such as** Tiktok, Facebook can *gain access to* *entertainment content* from *different religious and cultural backgrounds*. **This** can allow them to *have a better understanding of* *cultural differences*, **and therefore** they can become more *open-minded*. **The second advantage is that** <u>watching *educational programs*</u> on television can enable children to *broaden their knowledge*. **By way of illustration**, many children <u>who</u> *have a particular interest in* shows about nature <u>available on</u> channels **like** National Geographic can learn more about *wildlife*, **<u>thereby</u>** <u>eventually becoming</u> *more aware about* the importance of *protecting the environment*.

**In conclusion**, <u>despite</u> the *potential downsides* of children <u>using</u> *technologies* as their *leisure activities*, **they** *pale in comparison to* the *advantages involved*.
