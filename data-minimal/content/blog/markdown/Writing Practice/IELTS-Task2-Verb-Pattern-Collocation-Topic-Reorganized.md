### T1 — EDUCATION & HỌC TẬP

#### Advantages / Benefits

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">narrow the educational divide</span> | thu hẹp khoảng cách giáo dục | Investing in rural schools is the most effective way to **narrow the educational divide**. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">democratize education</span> | phổ cập hóa giáo dục | Online platforms **democratize education** by delivering university lectures to anyone with an internet connection. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">a greater depth of knowledge</span> | chiều sâu kiến thức lớn hơn | Specialising early allows undergraduates to acquire **a greater depth of knowledge** in their chosen field. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">personalized learning</span> | việc học cá nhân hóa | Adaptive software enables **personalized learning**, allowing each pupil to revisit topics they find difficult. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">learn at one's own pace</span> | học theo nhịp độ riêng | Recorded lectures allow students to **learn at their own pace** without falling behind their classmates. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">peer collaboration</span> | sự hợp tác giữa bạn học | **Peer collaboration** often clarifies difficult material more effectively than a lengthy teacher-led explanation. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">elucidate doubts</span> | làm rõ những thắc mắc | Small tutorial groups give learners frequent opportunities to **elucidate doubts** with their subject lecturers. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">minimize social distractions</span> | giảm thiểu các yếu tố gây phân tâm | Studying at home may **minimize social distractions**, but it also removes valuable peer interaction. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">foster the skills of independent learning and self-reliance</span> | hình thành kỹ năng tự học và tự lập | Boarding schools **foster the skills of independent learning and self-reliance** that universities increasingly demand. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">improve academic performance</span> | cải thiện kết quả học tập | Regular physical exercise has been shown to **improve academic performance** as well as general health. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">a conducive environment for doing sth</span> | môi trường thuận lợi để làm gì | Quiet, well-equipped libraries create **a conducive environment for concentrating on demanding academic work**. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">a harmonious and productive learning environment</span> | môi trường học tập hài hòa và hiệu quả | Clear behavioural rules are essential for maintaining **a harmonious and productive learning environment**. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">report lower levels of bullying</span> | ghi nhận mức độ bạo lực học đường thấp hơn | Schools with structured pastoral care consistently **report lower levels of bullying** than comparable institutions. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">a well-rounded individual</span> | một con người phát triển toàn diện | Education should aim to produce **a well-rounded individual** rather than merely a skilled examination candidate. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">foster creativity and well-rounded development</span> | nuôi dưỡng sự sáng tạo và phát triển toàn diện | Arts and music lessons **foster creativity and well-rounded development** that narrow academic subjects cannot. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">cultivate emotional intelligence</span> | nuôi dưỡng trí tuệ cảm xúc | Group projects **cultivate emotional intelligence** by requiring pupils to manage disagreement and shared responsibility. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">express ideas persuasively</span> | diễn đạt ý tưởng một cách thuyết phục | Debating societies teach adolescents to **express ideas persuasively** in front of a critical audience. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">improve time management skills</span> | cải thiện kỹ năng quản lý thời gian | Balancing coursework with part-time employment helps undergraduates **improve time management skills** considerably. |

#### Disadvantages / Drawbacks

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">widen learning gaps</span> | nới rộng khoảng cách học tập | Prolonged school closures **widened learning gaps** between wealthy pupils and their less affluent peers. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">erode critical thinking abilities</span> | làm xói mòn khả năng tư duy phản biện | Excessive reliance on artificial intelligence may **erode critical thinking abilities** among undergraduate students. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">undermine deep reading habits</span> | làm mai một thói quen đọc sâu | Constant scrolling through short articles can **undermine deep reading habits** developed during childhood. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">weaken long-term retention of knowledge</span> | làm giảm khả năng ghi nhớ lâu dài | Cramming before examinations tends to **weaken long-term retention of knowledge** across most academic subjects. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">a superficial understanding of + N</span> | sự hiểu biết nông cạn về | Students relying on video summaries often develop **a superficial understanding of climate science**. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">become disengaged and discouraged</span> | mất hứng thú và nhụt chí | Learners repeatedly compared with high achievers may **become disengaged and discouraged** within a single term. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">diminish motivation and learning outcomes</span> | làm giảm động lực và kết quả học tập | Excessive standardised testing can **diminish motivation and learning outcomes** across entire year groups. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">a decline in learning outcomes</span> | sự suy giảm kết quả học tập | Overcrowded classrooms have produced **a decline in learning outcomes** at many underfunded state schools. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">hinder academic performance</span> | cản trở thành tích học tập | Part-time employment during term time can seriously **hinder academic performance** among secondary school students. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">undermine academic integrity</span> | làm xói mòn tính liêm chính học thuật | Essay-writing services **undermine academic integrity** and devalue the qualifications that honest students earn. |

#### Causes / Reasons

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">strive for academic excellence</span> | nỗ lực đạt thành tích học tập xuất sắc | Pupils who constantly **strive for academic excellence** may neglect their physical and emotional wellbeing. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">study intensively for long hours</span> | học miệt mài nhiều giờ liền | Adolescents who **study intensively for long hours** frequently suffer from sleep deprivation and chronic anxiety. |

#### Problems / Challenges

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">repay student loans</span> | trả nợ vay sinh viên | Graduates who struggle to **repay student loans** often delay buying property or starting families. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">higher dropout rates</span> | tỉ lệ bỏ học cao hơn | Schools in impoverished districts consistently record **higher dropout rates** than those in affluent areas. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">overloaded curricula</span> | chương trình học quá tải | **Overloaded curricula** leave teenagers little time for sport, rest or genuine intellectual curiosity. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">face academic pressure</span> | đối mặt với áp lực học tập | Children in highly competitive systems **face academic pressure** from an unreasonably early age. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">academic dishonesty / the risk of plagiarism</span> | sự gian lận học thuật / nguy cơ sao chép | Universities employ detection software to reduce **the risk of plagiarism** in coursework submitted online. |

#### Solutions / Recommendations

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">fully subsidize tertiary education</span> | bao cấp toàn bộ giáo dục đại học | Governments that **fully subsidize tertiary education** must raise taxes or divert funding from other services. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">remove tuition fees</span> | bỏ học phí | Proposals to **remove tuition fees** entirely would place an enormous financial burden on national budgets. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">mitigate educational inequality</span> | giảm bất bình đẳng trong giáo dục | Free digital textbooks can **mitigate educational inequality** without imposing significant costs on the state. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">reduce disparities in learning opportunities</span> | giảm bất bình đẳng về cơ hội học tập | Well-funded public libraries **reduce disparities in learning opportunities** between urban and remote communities. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">reduce absenteeism</span> | giảm tình trạng nghỉ học | Providing free school meals has been shown to **reduce absenteeism** among primary pupils significantly. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">interactive teaching methods</span> | phương pháp dạy học tương tác | **Interactive teaching methods** encourage participation and make abstract scientific concepts far easier to grasp. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">project-based tasks / gamified lessons</span> | nhiệm vụ theo dự án / bài học được game hóa | Teachers increasingly use **project-based tasks** to help adolescents apply theory to authentic real-world problems. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">adjust the curriculum accordingly</span> | điều chỉnh chương trình học cho phù hợp | Schools should monitor labour market trends and **adjust the curriculum accordingly** every few years. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">study collaboratively in groups</span> | học tập theo nhóm | Pupils who **study collaboratively in groups** develop negotiation skills that prove valuable in future workplaces. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">promote lifelong learning</span> | thúc đẩy học tập suốt đời | Governments should **promote lifelong learning** by subsidising evening courses for workers of all ages. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">comprehensive orientation programs</span> | chương trình định hướng nhập học toàn diện | **Comprehensive orientation programs** help first-year students adapt quickly to unfamiliar academic expectations. |

### T2 — ENVIRONMENT & WILDLIFE

#### Disadvantages / Drawbacks

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">undermine long-term sustainability</span> | phá hoại tính bền vững lâu dài | Prioritising short-term economic growth inevitably **undermines long-term sustainability** in resource-dependent developing economies. |

#### Causes / Reasons

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be a major contributor to environmental degradation</span> | là nguyên nhân chính gây suy thoái môi trường | Intensive livestock farming **is a major contributor to environmental degradation** in tropical regions worldwide. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">result in / lead to substantial carbon emissions</span> | dẫn đến lượng khí thải carbon lớn / tăng cao | Long-haul air travel **results in substantial carbon emissions** that no individual offset scheme can neutralise. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">contribute to climate change</span> | góp phần gây biến đổi khí hậu | Deforestation in the Amazon basin continues to **contribute to climate change** on a global scale. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">accelerate global warming</span> | làm tăng nhanh sự nóng lên toàn cầu | Melting permafrost releases methane, which further **accelerates global warming** in a dangerous feedback loop. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">exacerbate the global climate crisis</span> | làm trầm trọng thêm khủng hoảng khí hậu | Continued subsidies for fossil fuels **exacerbate the global climate crisis** rather than resolving it. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be sent to landfills</span> | bị đưa ra bãi chôn lấp | Millions of tonnes of recyclable packaging **are sent to landfills** every year in wealthy countries. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">increase energy consumption and waste generation</span> | làm tăng tiêu thụ năng lượng và lượng rác thải | Rapid urbanisation tends to **increase energy consumption and waste generation** in already overburdened cities. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">lead to the destruction of natural habitats</span> | dẫn đến việc phá hủy môi trường sống tự nhiên | Expanding palm oil plantations **lead to the destruction of natural habitats** throughout Southeast Asia. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">urban expansion and deforestation</span> | mở rộng đô thị và phá rừng | **Urban expansion and deforestation** have eliminated vast tracts of woodland surrounding major tropical cities. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">carry profound environmental repercussions</span> | gây ra hậu quả môi trường sâu rộng | Large-scale dam construction can **carry profound environmental repercussions** for downstream farming communities. |

#### Problems / Challenges

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">environmental degradation</span> | sự suy thoái môi trường | Unregulated industrial growth has caused severe **environmental degradation** in many developing coastal regions. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">the (rapid) depletion of natural resources</span> | sự cạn kiệt (nhanh chóng) của tài nguyên thiên nhiên | Mass consumerism has accelerated **the rapid depletion of natural resources** across every inhabited continent. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">the emission of greenhouse gases</span> | sự phát thải khí nhà kính | Heavy industry remains responsible for **the emission of greenhouse gases** on an unprecedented scale. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">shifting weather patterns</span> | hình thái thời tiết thay đổi | **Shifting weather patterns** have already devastated harvests across large parts of sub-Saharan Africa. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">harmful contaminants</span> | các chất gây ô nhiễm độc hại | Untreated industrial effluent releases **harmful contaminants** into rivers that supply drinking water. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">hazardous levels of air pollution</span> | mức ô nhiễm không khí nguy hại | Several Asian capitals now experience **hazardous levels of air pollution** throughout the winter months. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">escalating pollution</span> | tình trạng ô nhiễm ngày càng leo thang | **Escalating pollution** in industrial zones has forced regulators to impose emergency factory closures. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">disrupt ecological balance / equilibrium</span> | phá vỡ cân bằng sinh thái | Introducing non-native species can **disrupt ecological equilibrium** in lakes and river systems permanently. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">fragile ecosystems</span> | những hệ sinh thái mong manh | Mass tourism threatens **fragile ecosystems** such as coral reefs and high-altitude mountain forests. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">biodiversity loss</span> | sự mất đa dạng sinh học | **Biodiversity loss** ultimately threatens agriculture, since crops depend on wild pollinators for survival. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">habitat destruction and overexploitation</span> | sự phá hủy môi trường sống và khai thác quá mức | **Habitat destruction and overexploitation** together account for most recorded extinctions during the past century. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">illegal timber harvesting</span> | việc khai thác gỗ trái phép | Satellite monitoring now helps authorities detect **illegal timber harvesting** in remote protected forests. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">expose flora and fauna to the risk of extinction</span> | đẩy hệ động thực vật vào nguy cơ tuyệt chủng | Climate-driven droughts **expose flora and fauna to the risk of extinction** in previously stable habitats. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">push many species closer to extinction</span> | đẩy nhiều loài đến bờ tuyệt chủng | Poaching for traditional medicine has **pushed many species closer to extinction** within a single generation. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">endangered populations</span> | các quần thể nguy cấp | Captive breeding programmes have successfully restored several **endangered populations** of large mammals. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">be deprived of their homes and food sources</span> | bị mất nơi ở và nguồn thức ăn | When forests are cleared, wild animals **are deprived of their homes and food sources**. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">transboundary challenges</span> | những thách thức vượt biên giới | River pollution and acid rain are **transboundary challenges** requiring coordinated international regulation. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">the environmental toll of + N</span> | tổn thất môi trường do | Few consumers appreciate **the environmental toll of fast fashion** on water supplies and soil. |

#### Solutions / Recommendations

| Cụm / Cấu trúc | Nghĩa | Ví dụ |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">prevent the overexploitation of natural resources</span> | ngăn việc khai thác quá mức tài nguyên | Strict fishing quotas **prevent the overexploitation of natural resources** in vulnerable coastal waters. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">curb emissions</span> | kìm hãm lượng khí thải | Governments cannot **curb emissions** effectively without simultaneously reforming transport and electricity generation. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">enforce stringent emission limits</span> | áp đặt hạn mức khí thải nghiêm ngặt | Only authorities willing to **enforce stringent emission limits** will meaningfully improve urban air quality. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">a national carbon tax</span> | thuế các-bon quốc gia | Introducing **a national carbon tax** would encourage manufacturers to adopt cleaner production processes. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">reduce one's carbon footprint</span> | giảm dấu chân carbon | Households can **reduce their carbon footprint** simply by insulating their homes and eating less meat. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">switch to renewable energy sources</span> | chuyển sang các nguồn năng lượng tái tạo | Nations that **switch to renewable energy sources** early will enjoy considerable long-term economic advantages. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">transition toward a circular economy</span> | chuyển dịch sang nền kinh tế tuần hoàn | Manufacturers must **transition toward a circular economy** in which materials are repeatedly recovered and reused. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">sustainable practices</span> | các hoạt động bền vững | Companies adopting **sustainable practices** often discover that efficiency savings outweigh the initial investment. |
| <span style="font-size: 1.08em;">eco-friendly consumer habits</span> | thói quen tiêu dùng thân thiện môi trường | Public awareness campaigns alone rarely translate into genuinely **eco-friendly consumer habits** among ordinary shoppers. |
