
## Các dạng câu hỏi

### Yes / No questions

| Mức độ | Tỉ lệ | Cụm từ gợi ý |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Yes** | **100%** | For sure / Definitely / Totally / Certainly / Without a doubt |
| | **60-70%** | Kind of / Probably / A little bit / I guess so / I would say so |
| **No** | **50/50** | I'm not sure / It's really hard to say / Maybe / I'm on the fence about this |
| | **- 60-70%** | Not really / I wouldn't say so / I guess not / Probably not / I wouldn't go out of my way to say yes |
| | **- 100%** | Not at all / Definitely not / Certainly not / I don't think so / The answer is a definite no |

**Mức độ 100%**

* Definitely / certainly $\leftrightarrow$ Definitely not / certainly not / the answer is a definite no
* Totally $\leftrightarrow$ Not at all

**Mức độ 60 - 70%**

* I guess so $\leftrightarrow$ I guess not
* Probably $\leftrightarrow$ Probably not
* I would say so $\leftrightarrow$ I wouldn't say so

## Patterns

### ✅ Positive Effects

*Subject SỰ VẬT / SỰ VIỆC (it / V-ing / Noun...)*
- allow someone to **V**
- help someone **V**
- encourage someone to **V**
- give someone the opportunity/the chance to **V**
- reduce the risk of **N / V-ing**
- increase the chance of **N / V-ing**
- give someone **N** / provide someone with **N** / equip someone with **N**

*Subject PRONOUN (I / You / They...)*
- have the opportunity / ability to **V**
- be able to **V**

> **Example**
> - Regular exercise **helps people stay** healthy.
> - Studying abroad **gives students the opportunity to experience** different cultures.

### ❌ Negative Effects

*Subject SỰ VẬT / SỰ VIỆC (it / V-ing / Noun...)*
- make someone **V**
- give someone no choice but to **V** / require someone to **V**
- cost money to **V** / take time / effort to **V**
- prevent / stop someone from **V-ing**
- discourage someone from **V-ing**
- distract someone from **Noun / V-ing**
- increase the risk of **N / V-ing**

*Subject PRONOUN (I / You / They...)*
- have to **V** / have no choice but to **V**
- can't help but have to **V**

> **Example**
> - Living in big cities can **make people suffer** from noise and pollution.
> - Heavy traffic can **stop people from getting** to work on time.

### Want / Need

- want to **V / N** / wish to **V** / hope to **V**
- need to **V / N**
- I wish to be able to **V**
- I am all about being able to **V** these days
- I truly long to **V**

### Feeling / Opinion Patterns

`Mở đầu:` I believe that / Personally / To me personally / From my perspective

| Loại chủ ngữ | Cấu trúc chính |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Người (I/They)** | S + V <br> find it + **Adj*** to **V** <br> consider **N** to be **Adj*** <br> consider / see **N1** (as) **N2** <br> think S should V <br> can't help but feel **Adj**** |
| **Sự vật (It/V-ing)** | It is **Adj** to **V** <br> make someone feel **Adj**** |

*\*Adj tính chất: beneficial, useful, convenient, difficult, boring...*
*\**Adj cảm giác: relaxed, confident, happy, exhausted, bored...*

### Preferences

*Subject SỰ VẬT / SỰ VIỆC (I / V-ing / Noun...)*
- is / are (not) my cup of tea
- is / are (not) my interest

*Subject NGƯỜI (I / They /...)*
- **➕** really like / enjoy **V-ing** / be (so) into something / be a (big) fan of something
- **➖** not too crazy about something / can't stand **V-ing**
- **↔️** 'd rather **V** than **V** / prefer something to something

> **Example**
> - Shopping is not really **my cup of tea**.
> - I **prefer** online newspapers **to** printed ones.

## Cách mở rộng câu trả lời

### I. Linear tool - Description

#### 1. Logic đằng sau Description - Specify information

**Specify Verb**
- What do you do?
- How (often/much) do you do it?

**Specify Objects**
- Who / what?
- What is it like?

**Specify Contexts**
- When?
- Where?
- With whom?

> **Example**
>
> **CÂU HỎI:** What do you do in your free time?
> **CÂU 1 (Answer):** It would be to listen to music.
>
> **CÂU 2+ (Description):**
> *   **Verb:** I always find a quiet corner in my house, get my headphones, then put on a nice song.
> *   **Object:** The music that I like most would be slow-paced instrumental songs.
> *   **Context:** I do this pretty much every night whenever I get to have some me-time.

#### 2. Kết nối Description với phần khung

**Trường hợp 1: Specify nouns**

`Structure (What, Who) + Pattern` ➔ `Vocab / Collocations`

*   `The NOUN that ... would be`
*   `The NOUN that ... would be (nothing else / no one else) (but / other than / if not)`
*   `There're a couple of NOUNs that ..., (one of which / out of which at the top) is N`

**Trường hợp 2: Specify verbs**

`Structure + Pattern` ➔ `Vocab / Collocations`

| Loại | Cấu trúc / Pattern |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Habit** | `('ve made / make) it a habit to V` <br> `make a habit out of V-ing` <br> `'ve habitualized V-ing` |
| **Preference** | `like it when I get to V` <br> `'d rather V than V` <br> `hate it when I have to V` |

### II. Linear tool - Cause and Effect

#### 1. Linear tool - Cause

**Đối tượng (Mối quan hệ khách quan)**
`Why` (Structure) + `Effect +/- / Opinion` (Pattern) ➔ `Vocab / Collocations` (Content)

**Bản thân (Mối quan hệ chủ quan)**
`Why` (Structure) + `Want / Need / Feeling` (Pattern) ➔ `Vocab / Collocations` (Content)

> **Example**
>
> **CÂU HỎI:** Do you like taking the bus to work / school?
> **CÂU 1 (Answer):** I really like it when I get to take the bus to school.
>
> **CÂU 2+ (Cause):**
> *   **+ Opinion:** The best part about it is that it allows me to take it slow and relax on my commute.
> *   **Feeling:** I find it to be the fastest and most environmentally-friendly option out there.

#### 2. Linear tool - Effect

**Bản thân (Mối quan hệ chủ quan) - Cấu trúc cảm xúc**
`That's why / As a result` (Structure) + `Want / Need / Preference / Feeling` (Pattern) ➔ `Vocab / Collocations` (Content)

**Bản thân (Mối quan hệ chủ quan) - Cấu trúc thói quen (Action-Habit)**
`That's why / As a result` (Structure) + `Habit...` (Pattern) ➔ `Vocab / Collocations` (Content)

> **Example**
>
> **CÂU HỎI:** Do you like taking the bus to work / school?
> **CÂU 1 (Answer):** I really like it when I get to take the bus to school.
>
> **CÂU 2+ (Effect):**
> *   **Feeling:** That's why I find myself always feeling quite happy with the wait that goes with it.
> *   **Action:** That's why I've never driven myself anywhere.

### III. 2 cách sử dụng Linear tools

**Linear tool ngang: bổ nghĩa Answer**

`Câu hỏi` → `Answer` → `Description` (Verb | Objects | Contexts) → `Cause` (Want | Feeling | +/- | Opinion) → `Effect` (Want | Feeling | Action - Habit)

> **Example**
>
> **CÂU HỎI:** Do you like taking the bus to work / school?
>
> **Flow 1: Answer → Description → Cause → Effect**
> 1.  **Answer:** I really like it when I get to take the bus.
> 2.  **Description:** The bus route that I take most often would be the one from home to school and back.
> 3.  **Cause:** This is because mom and dad are too busy to give me a ride in the morning.
> 4.  **Effect:** That's why they asked me to go with the buses.
>
> **Flow 2: Answer → Cause → Description**
> 1.  **Answer:** I really like it when I get to take the bus.
> 2.  **Cause:** The best part about buses is that it allows me to take it slow and relax on my commute.
> 3.  **Description:** The thing that I do is nothing if not to read a book that I love when I wait to get to school.

---

> **💡 Ghi chú: Cách phân biệt Want | Feeling giữa Cause và Effect**
>
> 1.  **Liên từ kết nối (Connectors):**
>     *   **Cause:** This is because..., The reason is...
>     *   **Effect:** That's why..., As a result...
> 2.  **Trình tự thời gian và logic hành động:**
>     *   **Cause:** Xảy ra **trước** hoặc **dẫn tới** hành động chính (Động cơ ➔ Hành động).
>     *   **Effect:** Xảy ra **sau** hoặc là **hệ quả trực tiếp** của hành động chính (Hành động ➔ Hệ quả).
> 3.  **Ý nghĩa thực tế:**
>     *   **Cause:** Trả lời cho "Tại sao làm".
>     *   **Effect:** Trả lời cho "Làm xong thì thế nào".

## Ứng dụng Linearthinking trong Part 1 & Part 3

### Cách ứng dụng Linearthinking® vào IELTS Speaking Part 1

`Part 1 Question` → `Answer` (Direct Answer) → `Description` (V|O|C) → `Cause` (W|F|+/-|O) → `Effect` (W|F|A)

> **Example**
>
> **01. What is your favourite holiday?**
>
> *   **Answer 1: Tết**
>     *   Flow: Description (not fixed date) ➔ Cause (interesting) ➔ Effect (look forward to it)
>     *   Sample: "My favorite holiday is definitely Tet... Usually falls between late January and mid-February. I find this variation quite interesting, which is why I always look forward to it every year."
> *   **Answer 2: Tết**
>     *   Flow: Cause (traditional customs) ➔ Effect (feel excited)
>     *   Sample: "Tet is my absolute favorite... because I get to participate in many traditional customs... That's why I always start to feel restless and excited whenever January arrives."

### Cách ứng dụng Linearthinking® vào IELTS Speaking Part 3

**Công cụ Linear tool thứ 4 - Example**

1.  **P3 / Difficult P1 questions:** `Question` → `Answer` → `Linear tool` → `Linear tool`
2.  **Take me / NOUN as an example:** `Question` → `Answer` → `Linear tool` (Example focus)

> **Example**
>
> **Câu hỏi: Do people change a lot?**
>
> *   **Phần 1: SOCIETY (Góc độ xã hội)**
>     *   Answer: Yes
>     *   Description: Expertise & mindset
>     *   Cause: Adapt to environments
> *   **Phần 2: YOU (Góc độ cá nhân)**
>     *   Marker: Take me as an example
>     *   Answer: Yes
>     *   Description: Emotionally stronger
>     *   Effect: No longer cry easily

### Tư duy Specify the Question

1.  Tìm thông tin chung chung / trừu tượng trong câu hỏi.
2.  Specify thông tin đó (Noun: things, people, action, place, time).
3.  Lên ý tưởng tập trung vào từng câu hỏi cụ thể.

**Sơ đồ tư duy:**
`Main Question` → `SPECIFY (Noun)` → `Branch 1/2 (Answer + Linear tools)`

> **Example: Advertising**
>
> | Aspect / Question | Specified Examples |
> | :--- | :--- |
> | What type of products? | Medicine, food, toys, clothes... |
> | What form of adverts? | Pop-ups, TV ads, billboards... |
> | What aspect of an ad? | Visual, sound, meaning, storyline... |

### Markers cho flow trả lời có Specify the question

*   **Marker 1 (Giới thiệu):** It (totally) depends on **NC** (we're talking about / really)
    *   *NC: Noun, the group/kind of N, how/what/where/when + SV*
*   **Marker 2 (Dẫn vào specified questions):**
    *   When it comes to N / If you look at N / Now, if we're talking about N
    *   I'd like to think/talk about N / It kinda causes me to think about N / First thing in my mind now is N

## Tư Duy Simplify & Think In English

### Bản chất simplify của tư duy Think in English

Think in English = Đơn giản hóa ý tưởng phức tạp ➔ diễn đạt bằng tiếng Anh cơ bản.

| Type | Ý TƯỞNG | THINK IN ENGLISH |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **VERB** | rước dâu | go to the bride's house and ask for her family's permission to take her home |
| | rước đèn trung thu | go around their neighbourhood together with a special type of lantern |
| **NOUN** | tiền lì xì | money that is put in a red envelope for good luck. |

### Cách Think in English - Verbs

`Verb không biết` ➔ `Verb đơn giản hơn` + `Bổ nghĩa (Object / Manner / Context / Purpose)`

**Formula for Verbs:**
- **Context:** prep + NOUN / where/when SV
- **Object:** NOUN / (that SV)
- **Manner:** adv / in a way that SV / with/without NOUN
- **Purpose:** (just) to + V / (just) so that SV

> **Example**
>
> | Động từ gốc | Verb đơn giản hơn | Bổ nghĩa thêm vào |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | tảo mộ | visit and clean up | to pay our respects (purpose) |
> | gian dối | say | things that are not true (object) |

### Cách Think in English - Nouns

`Noun không biết` ➔ `Noun chung chung` + `Bổ nghĩa (Description / Example)`

**Formula for Nouns:**
- **Noun:** this N, one of these Ns, a kinda N, a very special N
- **Description:** character (adj), action-habit (that V), relationship, length (for/since), importance (adj)
- **Example:** example, the likes of N, such as N

> **Example**
>
> | Danh từ gốc | Noun chung chung | Bổ nghĩa thêm vào |
> | :--- | :--- | :--- |
> | lễ vật | traditional gifts | including fruits, teas... (example) |
> | quan viên hai họ | people | from the bride and groom's families (description) |

#### Sample Answer

Oh, absolutely! You know, the first reason I can think of is that it really helps consumers make more informed decisions. With so many choices out there, advertising helps us figure out what's worth our time and money. It's like having a personal guide through the jungle of products! Another reason that I can think of is that advertising helps manufacturers build brand awareness and stand out in a competitive market. When you see their logo or hear their jingle, you instantly recognize them. It's all about creating a lasting impression.

**💡 Note từ vựng:**

*   **make more informed decisions**: đưa ra quyết định có căn cứ hơn
*   **with so many choices out there**: với vô vàn lựa chọn ngoài kia
*   **figure out what's worth our time and money**: xác định xem cái gì xứng đáng với thời gian và tiền bạc của mình
*   **a personal guide through the jungle of products**: người hướng dẫn cá nhân trong "mê rừng" sản phẩm
*   **build brand awareness**: xây dựng nhận thức thương hiệu
*   **stand out in a competitive market**: nổi bật trong thị trường cạnh tranh
*   **logo / jingle**: logo / nhạc hiệu quảng cáo
*   **instantly recognize**: nhận ra ngay lập tức
*   **create a lasting impression**: tạo ấn tượng lâu dài
