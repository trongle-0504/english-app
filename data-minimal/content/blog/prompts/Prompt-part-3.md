#### Sample Answer

It's crucial for advertisers to tell the whole truth in advertisements. The way people can do this is to provide their customers with honest information about their products, like the ingredients or the side effects. The reason why people should be telling the truth is that this will allow companies to build up trust with their customers. When companies try to trick their customers, the result could be a damaged brand and the loss of loyal customers. Take the Vietnamese honey industry as an example. When the news about the many companies in the industry were producing synthetic honey, the customers immediately turned their backs on those companies.
